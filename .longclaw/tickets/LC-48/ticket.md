**An in-app route to the documentation.** Carried as an open decision through two candidates

## Why now

A user who installed the DMG has no route to the guide, because the app ships without a shell or URL-opening capability *by design* — the privacy boundary `release:audit` enforces. So this is a design question, not an oversight: ship the guide into new projects, render it in-app, or accept repository-only
