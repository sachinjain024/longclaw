The welcome screen gets a human as far as a project and a first ticket. After that, most of what LongClaw can do is sitting in a shortcut, a palette row, a settings pane or a file on disk, and nothing on an ordinary launch points at it. People who already have a project open still miss the CLI, agent setup, attachments, board keys, filter, archive, and the rest of the surface they would use every day.

Show one short tip each time the app starts with a project open: a rotating deck of shipped capabilities, each with a name, one sentence, and the shortcut or address that finds it. The spec holds the candidate list, the first deck, and the rules for when a tip appears.

## Checklist

- [ ] Settle the surface: placement, cadence, dismiss, persistence <!-- longclaw:item=ck_a1b24e15 -->
- [ ] Review and cut the feature deck copy <!-- longclaw:item=ck_bddb717c -->
- [ ] Build a prototype with scenes and a copy deck <!-- longclaw:item=ck_6953c2d0 -->
- [ ] Write settled copy into the ticket after prototype review <!-- longclaw:item=ck_e5d66726 -->
